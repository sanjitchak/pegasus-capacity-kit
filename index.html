Before you download

Who is this kit for?

Independent US P&C agency owners and operations managers, especially smaller teams managing an active book and recurring servicing work. It is an operations resource, not a guide to buying insurance.

What exactly will I receive?

A free 14-page PDF covering seven time leaks and their growth connections, plus a fillable workload worksheet, task handoff brief and capacity-to-growth plan. You can save it, fill it in with a compatible PDF reader or print it.

Do I need to book a call?

No. The kit is yours to use without a call or purchase. The free growth capacity review is an optional next step if you want help connecting one workflow to a growth priority.

Does this guarantee more revenue or hours saved?

No. The kit helps you plan and measure. Revenue depends on qualified opportunities, follow-up, conversion, retention and execution. Released time creates capacity; your team must put it to productive use.

What happens to my information?

We record your name, work email, phone number and whether you work solo, with a team or are just starting out when you request the kit. The download opens immediately after a successful request. This form does not subscribe you to email marketing. See our privacy notice.
